Hidden Gems

The Waterfall Capital

Labo isn’t just nature — it’s a haven of waterfalls waiting to be explored. From hidden tiered cascades to serene family-friendly spots, every trek leads you deeper into untouched beauty:

  • Turayog Falls
    Known for its impressive height and cool basin; a must-visit for trekkers.
  • Binuang Falls
    A secluded paradise perfect for adventurous hikers and nature lovers.
  • Malatap Falls
    Multi-tiered waterfall with shallow basins ideal for swimming and picnics.
Agricultural Pride

The Queen Pineapple

Labo is one of the key producers of the Queen Formosa Pineapple, the sweetest pineapple variety widely grown in Camarines Norte. Known for its intense sweetness, aromatic flavor, and crisp texture, this variety is a vital agricultural treasure with local processing into juices, jams, and dried products.

Annual Celebrations

Year-Round Festivities

Labo comes alive throughout the year with vibrant celebrations that showcase our community spirit and gratitude for our blessings.

January
New Year Community Celebrations

The year begins with thanksgiving masses, family gatherings, and community celebrations as Labo welcomes the new year with hope and unity.

May
Flores de Mayo

A month-long floral procession honoring the Virgin Mary. Witness daily parades with flower-adorned floats and local devotion.

September
Busig-on Festival

Our most celebrated event (Sept 1-8). Epic street dancing, historical reenactments, and live entertainment showcase Bicolano pride.

December

Living Heritage

Culture & Traditions

Bicolano Spirit

Labo's culture is deeply rooted in Bicolano heritage. Our people are known for their resilience, creativity, and strong family values. We take pride in our indigenous crafts, oral traditions, and the legendary Epic of Busig-on.

  • Oral Storytelling: Ancient tales preserved through community gatherings.
  • Textile Weaving: Intricate abaca products reflecting agricultural heritage.
  • Traditional Crafts: Bamboo products, pottery, and wood carving.
  • Family-First Values: Bayanihan spirit of mutual aid and cooperation.

The Epic of Busig-on

"In the mountains and rivers of Labo, a hero named Busig-on lives on in local oral tradition. His story, passed down through storytelling, song, and festival performances, reflects Bicolano values of courage, justice, and devotion to family."

Traditional Music

Bamboo instruments, drums, and folk songs commonly performed during festivals and rituals.

Traditional Cuisine

Authentic dishes like Bicol Express, fresh seafood, and unique pineapple-infused delicacies.

Local Arts & Crafts

Master craftspeople creating traditional textiles, baskets, and wooden sculptures.

Bayanihan Spirit

Communal unity where neighbors help each other in harvests and projects without reward.

Oral Traditions

Elders sharing wisdom through stories, riddles (bugtong), and proverbs.

Spiritual Heritage

Deep Catholic faith blended with indigenous beliefs creating unique religious celebrations.

Preserving History

Museo de Labo

Museo de Labo showcases treasured pieces of the municipality’s past, from traditional cultural artifacts to local heritage items that reflect Labo’s story through the ages. A visit here offers insight into the town’s history and identity.

LaboConnect for Business

Why Invest in Labo?

Labo is a 1st Class Municipality and a dynamic center of trade, agriculture, and investment opportunities in Camarines Norte.

Strategic Location

Situated at the heart of Camarines Norte, Labo connects to major routes and serves as a hub for commerce and logistics in the province.

Agri‑Industrial Strength

One of the top producers of coconut, copra, abaca, rice, and pineapple, with vast agricultural lands providing reliable raw materials for industry.

Mineral Resources

Abundant natural resources including gold and other minerals present opportunities in responsible mining and value‑added processing industries.

Skilled & Available Workforce

A labor pool engaged in agriculture, small-scale manufacturing, crafts, and emerging services ready to support diverse business ventures.

Agri‑Entrepreneurship Support

Local initiatives like support for young farmers and technology incubation hubs promote sustainable agricultural innovation and enterprise growth.

Tourism Potential

Eco‑tourism assets including waterfalls, mountains, and cultural festivals offer hospitality, service, and adventure investment opportunities.

Growing Connectivity

Expanding digital infrastructure supports online business, remote work, and emerging digital enterprises in the region.

Lifestyle & Quality of Life

Clean, safe, and accessible environment combined with vibrant culture, festivals, and natural beauty makes Labo ideal for wellness, retreats, and remote work hubs.
